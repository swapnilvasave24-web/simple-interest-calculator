# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description
The Simple Interest Calculator calculates the simple interest and total amount based on the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period.

## Formula

**Simple Interest (SI) = (P × R × T) / 100**

Where:
- **P** = Principal amount
- **R** = Annual rate of interest (in percentage)
- **T** = Time period (in years)

**Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest**

## Example

Suppose:
- Principal = ₹10,000
- Rate of Interest = 5% per year
- Time = 2 years

Calculation:

SI = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100

SI = ₹1,000

Total Amount = ₹10,000 + ₹1,000 = ₹11,000

- Calculates the total amount payable.
